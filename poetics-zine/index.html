POETICS

Poetry Zine

POETICS is a free poetry zine from Bainbridge Island Press. A new issue comes out every two months, gathering poems into a pocket-sized chapbook made to be carried, shared, and left for the next reader.

Copies are placed around Bainbridge Island—in libraries, cafés, and other public spots— as part of our commitment to poetry as a living public art. Browse past issues below, or download a PDF to read anywhere.
